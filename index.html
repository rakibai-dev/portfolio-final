The Feature

Bachelor’s Thesis

Robotic Automation

Teaching a UR3 to avoid the obstacle — reinforcement learning, from simulation to steel.

A distributed ROS 2 ecosystem, a carefully engineered reward function, and a policy that made the leap from Gazebo to physical hardware.

I architected a distributed ROS 2 node ecosystem managing continuous joint-space telemetry, integrating custom Python publishers to bridge Gazebo with a Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) training loop.

I iteratively tuned PPO network hyperparameters to maximize convergence stability within complex, high-dimensional state and action spaces, an exercise in patient, methodical debugging.

“Rigorous mathematical penalties for velocity saturation and integral windup — safety, written into the reward.”

I engineered a dynamic reward function that enforces industrial safety constraints, proactively applying rigorous mathematical penalties for velocity saturation and integral windup.

Finally, I executed a successful sim-to-real policy transfer to physical UR3 hardware, using tf2 transforms and the ros2_control framework to keep ML inference precisely synchronized with physical actuation.

  • Gazebo
  • PPO
  • Python
